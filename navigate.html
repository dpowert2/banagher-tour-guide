Athlone Lock

Checking…

The only lock on your route — and you pass it twice: northbound to Lough Ree on Saturday, southbound back on Sunday. Keeper-operated, no VHF. August hours 09:30–19:30 Mon–Sat, 09:30–18:00 Sun, closed 13:00–14:00 for lunch. €1.50 by smart card or cash. Gates open outside these hours only in exceptional circumstances.

Lock-keeper: 090 649 2026 · Silverline: 057 915 1112 · Emergency: 112

Waypoints — tap to target

Rules of the road: keep red markers to starboard heading upstream (north), green to port; reverse coming back. 5 knots through towns and past moored boats. Bridge arches at Shannonbridge and Banagher are narrow — line up early and commit.

Lough Ree: the only genuinely exposed water. Cross in the morning, keep the marked channel between Athlone and Hodson Bay, and don't cut corners near the islands — they have shoals off them.

Distances are straight-line, so they read short against the river's meander — expect the actual run to be 15–25% longer. ETA assumes 9 km/h.
